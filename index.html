I'm stuck. Where should I start?

Confirm the Lakehouse names and dbo schema against the architecture guide. Run notebooks in order, and read the validation section at the end of your current goal. For sign-in, permissions, capacity, or source-connection issues, ask your facilitator. Compare with reference notebooks after trying the starter.

Do I need to complete the optional Foundry path?

No. Use either built-in Fabric AI functions in notebook 3 or the alternate Foundry path in 3a. If you choose Foundry, update appeal_ai_source_table in notebook 4 as described in Goal 4.

A safe place to experiment. All property data is synthetic. AI outputs require human review and must not decide real assessments, taxes, or appeals. Keep account and source credentials out of notebooks, screenshots, and Git.
